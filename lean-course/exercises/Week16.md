# Week 16 exercise: Bonus: categories and functors

**Format:** 90-minute exercise session. Work in `Templates/Week16.lean`; compare with `Course/Week16.lean` after attempting the tasks. The bonus task is optional.

## 1. Read and predict (20 minutes)

State the identity and associativity laws for function composition. Explain what the objects and arrows are in the type/function example.

## 2. Program (45 minutes)

Complete `compose_assoc` with `funext`. Prove the identity and composition laws for `Option.map`.

## 3. Explain or prove (25 minutes)

Read each Lean proof and identify the mathematical law it establishes. Explain why a type-correct `map` function might still fail a functor law.

## Bonus

Define `Tree.map` and prove that it preserves composition.
